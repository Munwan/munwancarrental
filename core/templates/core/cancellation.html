Full Policy Details

✅
Full Refund — Cancel 48+ hours before pick-up
100% Refunded
Cancel more than 48 hours before your scheduled pick-up time and receive a full 100% refund to your original payment method within 3–5 business days. M-Pesa refunds are sent to the originating number within 48 hours.
⚠️
80% Refund — Cancel within 24 hours of pick-up
80% Refunded
If you cancel within 24 hours of pick-up — including same-day cancellations and no-shows — you still receive an 80% refund. The remaining 20% covers preparation, fuel, and operational costs already incurred. We never charge you the full amount regardless of how late the cancellation is.
✏️
Free Date & Time Changes
No Charge
You may change pick-up and return dates or times free of charge up to 24 hours before pick-up, subject to vehicle availability. Upgrades may incur a difference in daily rate.

  • Refunds are always returned to the original payment method — no cash payments.
