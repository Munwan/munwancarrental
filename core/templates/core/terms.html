1. Eligibility & Driver Requirements

To rent a vehicle from Munwan Car Rental, the customer (and any additional driver) must:

  • Be at least 23 years old with a minimum of 2 years' driving experience. Drivers under 25 may pay a young-driver surcharge.
  • Hold a valid, unrestricted driving licence — Kenyan residents: national licence; international visitors: national licence plus a valid International Driving Permit (IDP).
  • Present a valid passport or national ID at pickup for identification.
  • Not be disqualified from driving by any court or medical authority.
  • Provide a mobile number reachable during the rental period.

Only drivers registered with Munwan Car Rental at booking may operate the vehicle. Adding another driver mid-rental requires written approval.

2. Vehicle Use & Restrictions

The vehicle may only be used for lawful, private transportation within Kenya, subject to these conditions:

  • Unlimited mileage applies within Kenya. The vehicle must remain within Kenyan borders for the entire rental period — cross-border travel is not permitted.
  • The vehicle must not be used for: commercial ride-sharing (Uber, Bolt, etc.), towing (unless vehicle specified), motor-sport, off-road use outside designated parks, instruction, or sub-hiring to third parties.
  • Smoking is prohibited in all vehicles.
  • Carrying more passengers than the vehicle's licensed capacity is prohibited.
  • Any traffic fines, parking tickets, or toll-road charges incurred during the rental period are the customer's responsibility.

3. Insurance & Customer Liability

All Munwan Car Rental rentals include comprehensive motor insurance:

  • Third-party liability cover (bodily injury and property damage).
  • Theft protection (subject to police report and keys being returned).
  • Passenger accident cover.

Standard excess applies in the event of accident, damage, or theft — typically KES 50,000–150,000 depending on vehicle class. Customers may purchase a zero-excess upgrade at checkout (approx. $8/day).

Insurance will not apply if:

  • The driver is under the influence of alcohol or narcotics (above legal limit).
  • The vehicle is driven by an unapproved person.
  • Damage occurs during illegal or reckless use.
  • The customer leaves the accident scene without reporting.

In the event of any accident or theft, the customer must immediately call Munwan Car Rental on +254 727 745 907 and file a police report within 24 hours.

4. Rental Rates & Payment

  • Daily rates are based on a 24-hour period from pickup time. Minimum rental period is 2 days. Hire type affects daily pricing (corporate hire, long-term and safari packages each use distinct rates — the total shown at checkout is final).
  • No security deposit is required. Your booking total is what you pay — no surprise hold on your card or M-Pesa.
  • Accepted payment methods: M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard, Verve (via Paystack), and PayPal. Payments may be made in USD, KES, EUR or GBP.
  • Fuel is not included. Vehicles are delivered with a full tank and must be returned full. Refuelling fees apply: pump rate + KES 500 service charge.
  • Late payment of any charge attracts interest at 2% per month plus reasonable recovery costs.

5. Vehicle Pickup & Return

  • Vehicles are inspected by Munwan Car Rental and the customer at pickup. Any existing damage is noted on the rental agreement — please point out anything we miss.
  • The vehicle must be returned to the agreed location, at the agreed date and time, in the same condition (reasonable wear-and-tear accepted).
  • Late return: a grace period of 1 hour applies. Beyond that, an additional day's rental is charged per 24-hour period of overdue time.
  • Early return does not entitle the customer to a pro-rata refund unless Munwan Car Rental agrees in writing.
  • Loss of vehicle keys is charged at KES 10,000 plus the cost of locksmith/replacement.

6. Cancellations & Modifications

Cancellations are governed by our separate Cancellation Policy, which forms part of these Terms. We always refund — no zero-refund situations:

  • 48+ hours before pickup → 100% refund
  • Within 24 hours of pickup (including same-day cancellation and no-shows) → 80% refund
  • Date and time changes are free up to 24 hours before pickup, subject to availability.

7. Privacy & Data Protection

Munwan Car Rental collects personal information (name, email, phone, licence details, payment information) only to process your booking, meet legal obligations, and provide customer support. We:

  • Do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.
  • Store card details only via PCI-DSS compliant processors (Paystack, PayPal) — never on our own servers.
  • Use live GPS tracking on all vehicles for safety, recovery, and operational purposes.
  • Retain booking records for a minimum of 7 years as required by Kenyan tax law.

You may request access to or deletion of your data by emailing info@munwancarrental.com.
