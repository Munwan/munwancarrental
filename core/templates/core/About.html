About Munwan Car Rental — Trusted Car Rental in Nairobi

Kenya's friendliest car hire company — serving locals, tourists, business travellers and safari explorers since 2018.

Our Story

Built in Nairobi, for Everyone Who Drives in Kenya

Munwan Car Rental was founded in Nairobi in 2018 with one simple mission: to make car rental in Kenya easy, affordable, and welcoming for everyone — whether you're a Kenyan resident heading upcountry, a business traveller attending a conference, or an international tourist arriving at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) for the first time.

What started as a small fleet of five vehicles has grown into one of Kenya's most trusted car hire providers, with a carefully maintained fleet of 12+ vehicles ranging from economy saloons like the Toyota Fielder to rugged safari-ready 4x4s like the Toyota Land Cruiser Prado. Every vehicle is fully insured, GPS-tracked, and serviced to the highest standards.
